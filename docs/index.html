About This Platform

IAS Watch — Western Province River Basins

A participatory Web-GIS platform developed as part of the Regional Planning and Design Studio programme to support community monitoring, ecological risk assessment and evidence-based decision-making for invasive alien plant species management in Sri Lanka's Western Province.

🎯 Platform Objectives

  • Map current IAS distribution using satellite imagery and field data
  • Enable anonymous community reporting of new sightings
  • Provide planners with decision-support analytics
  • Raise public awareness of riparian ecosystem threats
  • Build an open, continuously updated spatial dataset

🌍 Study Area

The platform covers three major river basins in Sri Lanka's Western Province: the Kelani River (145 km, draining into Colombo), the Kalu River (129 km, draining at Kalutara), and the Attanagalu Oya (72 km). Combined basin area: 847 km².

👥 Who Can Use This

This platform is publicly accessible for community members, researchers, planners, local government, and conservation groups. No account is required to submit a sighting. Submitted details are reviewed privately by the administrator; verified observations appear in Insights.

📊 Data & Privacy

Community reports are saved in the web map's private database, with optional photographs in private storage. Only administrator-verified observation fields are shown publicly in Insights. The 160 synthetic sample candidates are kept separate until documented field verification.

Multispectral bands B4 (Red) and B8 (NIR) used for NDVI computation and vegetation health assessment.

Species identification keys and ecological impact assessments for documented IAS in Sri Lanka.

Hydrology

River basin delineations, river network shapefiles and administrative boundaries for Western Province.

Ecology

CEA Sri Lanka — IAS Reports

Central Environmental Authority data on invasive species distribution and management priorities, 2018–2024.

Research

GBIF Species Occurrence Data

Global Biodiversity Information Facility occurrences for Sri Lanka IAS, used to validate classification outputs.

Land Cover

10m global land cover classification used for riparian habitat delineation and change detection baseline.

Supplementary water quality monitoring stations along Kelani and Kalu rivers, 2022–2024.

📚 Academic Context

This platform was developed as part of the Regional Planning and Design Studio programme. The project applies participatory GIS methodologies to environmental planning, integrating remote sensing data with community-sourced spatial observations.

For technical inquiries or collaboration, contact the project team through your university department. All source code and GEE scripts are available upon request.
